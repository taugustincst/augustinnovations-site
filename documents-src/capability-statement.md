## Core competencies

- **Healthcare software development and operation** — design, build, and hosted operation of production clinical and program-management software.
- **Substance-use disorder (SUD) program data systems** — outreach, harm-reduction supply tracking, referrals, consent management, and state reporting workflows.
- **Security testing for healthcare software** — white/gray-box web application penetration testing under signed authorization, delivered through the Clinical Perimeter practice.
- **Clinical de-identification tooling** — local-only software that replaces identifiers in clinical documents with consistent synthetic data for test, demo, and training use.
- **Healthcare AI deployment consulting** — evaluating, piloting, and rolling out AI tools inside live clinical environments, including EHR workflow optimization, reporting, and staff training.

## Products and services

- **SUDS** — hosted data platform for California harm-reduction and substance-use disorder programs: street outreach, naloxone and supplies tracked by lot, referrals, 42 CFR Part 2 consent workflows, and CalOMS and opioid-settlement reporting. Version 1.26.0 is live in production. (sudsdata.com)
- **Clinical Perimeter** — web application penetration tests for healthcare software vendors, performed by AugustInnovations LLC dba Clinical Perimeter: findings report, attestation letter, remediation call, and one retest within 30 days. (clinicalperimeter.com)
- **CSeed** — local-only Windows application that de-identifies clinical documents (DOCX, PDF with OCR, TXT, CSV, HL7, FHIR) and seeds consistent synthetic identities in their place; crosswalk protected with AES-256-GCM encryption. Current version 1.0.1.
- **Forward-deployed healthcare AI consulting** — AI deployment, EHR workflow optimization, reporting builds, and role-based training for clinics and health organizations.

## Differentiators

- **Practitioner-operated:** the operator is a practicing Clinical Application Specialist II working in a live clinic environment — the software and advice are grounded in current frontline workflows, not theory.
- **Production-proven in-house:** SUDS v1.26.0 runs in production under AugustInnovations' own operation, with encryption at rest, audit logging, and single-tenant isolation (a separate server, instance, database, keys, and subdomain per customer).
- **Regulated-industry discipline:** signed authorization before any security testing, staging and synthetic data only, 42 CFR Part 2 consent workflows built into the flagship product.
- **Senior expertise on every engagement:** no hand-offs to junior staff — scoping, delivery, and support come from the same senior operator.
