# Clinical Perimeter — Service Sheet

**AugustInnovations LLC dba Clinical Perimeter** · hello@clinicalperimeter.com · clinicalperimeter.com

## The problem

Your buyers — clinics, health systems, and enterprise customers — are asking how you protect the patient data in your application, and "we take security seriously" is no longer an answer. A HIPAA risk analysis or SOC 2 program needs evidence: an independent, documented penetration test of the application itself.

## The service

One web application, tested **white-box/gray-box** in your **staging environment with synthetic data only**:

- Authentication and session handling
- Authorization and role separation (including clinical vs. administrative roles)
- Input validation and injection
- Business logic
- API endpoints
- Healthcare-specific exposure checks — protected health information appearing in responses, exports, error messages, and logs

Methodology is checklist-driven from the **OWASP Web Security Testing Guide (WSTG)**.

## What you receive

1. **Full findings report** — every finding with severity, evidence, reproduction steps, and remediation guidance written for your engineers.
2. **Attestation letter** — a one-page letter on letterhead stating that the testing was performed, the methodology used, and the scope covered — suitable for buyer and auditor requests.
3. **60-minute remediation call** — we walk your team through the findings and the fix order.
4. **One retest within 30 days** — fixes are verified and the attestation is updated.

## Engagement terms

- **Flat-fee engagement** — one price, scoped and agreed in writing before work begins
- Report delivered within **10 business days** of the testing window closing
- Engagements are scheduled through **hello@clinicalperimeter.com**

## Guardrails (non-negotiable)

- **No testing without signed authorization.** A signed Rules of Engagement and written client authorization are executed before any testing begins — every time.
- **Staging and synthetic data only.** Production systems and any system containing real patient data are never tested.
- **Confidential handling.** Evidence is stored encrypted, access-limited, and retained per the engagement agreement.

## Not included

Production systems or any system containing real patient data; denial-of-service or load testing; social engineering or phishing; physical security; internal-network, wireless, or mobile-application testing.
