About

One operator, deep bench of scar tissue

AugustInnovations was founded in 2022 by Taylor Augustin, a Clinical Application Specialist II whose career runs from the operating room (twelve years as a Certified Surgical Technologist) through Tier 3 enterprise EHR support across dozens of hospitals, to building production software with generative AI. The company is deliberately small: the person who scopes your work is the person who does it, and subcontractors — used when a job needs them — are always disclosed.

We hold ourselves to the rules of the most regulated room we work in: signed authorization before testing, synthetic data wherever possible, encryption at rest, audit trails, and claims we can show our work for.
