SUDS — Vendor Overview for Buyers

AugustInnovations LLC · info@suds.systems · sudsdata.com Prepared: October 23, 2026

What SUDS is

SUDS is the hosted data platform for California harm-reduction and substance-use disorder (SUD) programs. It runs the daily work of a program in one system:

  • Street outreach encounters — named or anonymous, with offline capture
  • Naloxone and supply distribution tracked by lot
  • Referrals and follow-up
  • Consent management built for 42 CFR Part 2
  • Reporting for CalOMS Tx and opioid-settlement requirements

SUDS is built and operated by AugustInnovations LLC, a California LLC (Entity No. 202253018246; D-U-N-S 12-343-3746) whose operator is a practicing Clinical Application Specialist II.

Deployment model

SUDS is multi-tenant software operated by AugustInnovations on one managed fleet — with strict per-tenant isolation:

  • Each tenant organization gets its own instance/process, its own database, its own encryption keys, and its own subdomain
  • Tenants never share a database with another organization
  • Hosting is included in every tier; there is nothing for the buyer to install or maintain

Security posture

  • Encryption at rest for tenant data
  • Tamper-evident audit logging of access and changes
  • 42 CFR Part 2 consent workflows enforced in the application, not left to policy
  • Role-based access with multi-factor authentication
  • An internal security self-assessment has been completed and its findings remediated; an independent third-party penetration test is in procurement (vendor proposals due October 27, 2026, testing window November 2026). AugustInnovations does not claim a completed independent penetration test until that engagement concludes.
